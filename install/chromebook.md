# Install on a Chromebook

[← Pick a different computer](../INSTALL.md)

### [**▶ Open Smart School Tool**](https://notisaac132-star.github.io/Isaac-s-Smart-School-Tool/app/)

## Steps

1. **Open the app.** Click **Open Smart School Tool** above. It opens in Chrome.
2. **Install it.** Use either one:
   - Click the **⬇ Install app** button at the bottom of the screen, **or**
   - Click the **install icon** (a little computer with a down arrow) at the right end of the address bar.

   No install option? Click Chrome's **⋮** menu (top-right) → **Cast, save, and share** → **Install page as app…**
3. **Click Install** in the box that pops up.
4. **Done!** Smart School Tool opens in its own window. You'll find it in your **launcher** (the circle in the bottom-left corner). To keep it on your shelf, right-click the icon on the shelf → **Pin**.
5. **Make your account.** Click **Sign up**, enter your name, email and a password, then add your teacher's email.

## Updates

Nothing to do. Every time you open the app (with internet), it loads the newest version by itself. The version number is
at the bottom-left of the home screen.

## Having trouble?

- **School Chromebook won't let you install apps?** Your school may block installing. The app works in a normal Chrome
  tab too: open the link and press **Ctrl + D** to bookmark it.
- **It opened but I can't log in?** Logging in needs internet. Check that you're online and try again.
- **Want to remove it?** Right-click the icon in the launcher → **Uninstall**. Your account and study log stay saved.
