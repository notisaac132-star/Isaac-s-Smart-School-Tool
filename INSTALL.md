**Not sure what you have?**
- **Chromebook:** the keyboard has a 🔍 search key where Caps Lock usually is, and the bottom-right corner shows the time and your profile picture.
- **Windows:** there's a ⊞ Windows logo on the Start button and on the keyboard.
- **Mac:** there's an Apple logo in the top-left corner of the screen.

The app is free, and every version keeps itself up to date after you install it.
