# Install on Windows

[← Pick a different computer](../INSTALL.md)

### [**⬇ Download the installer (.exe)**](https://github.com/notisaac132-star/Isaac-s-Smart-School-Tool/releases/latest/download/Smart-School-Tool-Windows-Setup.exe)

## Steps

1. **Download it.** Click **Download the installer** above. Your browser saves *Smart-School-Tool-Windows-Setup.exe*,
   usually in your **Downloads** folder.
2. **Open the file.** Click it in your browser's downloads list, or double-click it in **Downloads**.
3. **"Windows protected your PC"?** This blue box appears because the app isn't signed with a paid certificate.
   Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
4. **Follow the installer.** Click **Next**. You can keep the suggested folder. Then click **Install**, then **Finish**.
5. **Open it.** Smart School Tool is now in your **Start menu** and on your **desktop**.
6. **Make your account.** Click **Sign up**, enter your name, email and a password, then add your teacher's email.

## Updates

Nothing to do. The app checks for updates by itself, downloads them in the background, and asks you to restart when a new
version is ready (or updates the next time you close it). The version number is in the window's title bar.

## Can't install programs?

Use the **portable version**: one file you just double-click, with no installing.
[**Download the portable version (.exe)**](https://github.com/notisaac132-star/Isaac-s-Smart-School-Tool/releases/latest/download/Smart-School-Tool-Windows-Portable.exe).
It tells you when there's a new version and opens the download for you.

## Having trouble?

- **No "Run anyway" button?** Click **More info** first. It's a small link under the message.
- **Your antivirus blocked it?** Allow *Smart School Tool* in your antivirus, then open the file again.
- **Want to remove it?** **Start** → **Settings** → **Apps** → **Installed apps** → **Smart School Tool** → **Uninstall**.
  Your account and study log stay saved.
