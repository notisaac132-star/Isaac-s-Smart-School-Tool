Smart School Tool

Keep your teachers' and tutors' emails in one place.

Download for Windows

Download installer (.exe) Portable version (no install)
  1. Open the downloaded file.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.

Download for Mac

Download for Mac (.dmg) Download as .zip
  1. Open the .dmg and drag Smart School Tool into Applications.
  2. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app. Click Done, then go to Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. No Open Anyway button? Open Terminal, paste this and press Enter:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Smart School Tool.app"
  4. Works on both Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3…) and Intel Macs.
